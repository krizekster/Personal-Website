## Overview

Games initiate and sustain the psychological flow state more reliably than almost any other interactive medium. They achieve this primarily through dynamic difficulty balancing — maintaining a continuous feedback loop that tightly tethers challenge to skill.

## Key Thesis

- **Systematizing Flow:** Maps the precise interaction mechanisms behind game immersion and connects them to Cal Newport's principles of deep work.
- **Environmental Attention:** Examines ADHD hyperfocus in well-designed game environments as empirical evidence that environment and feedback pacing dictate attention quality.
- **Practical Application:** Formulates a systematic methodology for exporting focus mechanics from game loops into everyday work, learning, and productivity tasks.
