## Overview

The formal specification of tGiX™ (Telemetry-based Gamified Interaction Index): a non-intrusive, platform-agnostic framework that intercepts high-frequency interaction telemetry and translates it into citable cognitive performance metrics.

## Technical Architecture

- **The Altered Quotient (0.00 to 100.00):** Eight weighted vectors resolving into a single unified composite score measuring cognitive energy and effort rather than simple win/loss outcomes.
- **Mathematical Governors:** Integrates a logistic growth multiplier, logarithmic streak progression, linear streak decay, and Fibonacci progression spacing.
- **The Equalizer:** A non-linear stabilising function ensuring single exceptional sessions cannot artificially inflate index reliability.
- **Dual-Tier Ingestion:** Intercepts static inputs (sessions, articles, XP, streaks) alongside dynamic playback diagnostics (decision velocity, focus ratios, completion duration).
- **Metacognitive Layer:** Introduces Codename TGX-1, a fine-tuned small language model acting as an automated metacognitive interpreter over interaction telemetry.
