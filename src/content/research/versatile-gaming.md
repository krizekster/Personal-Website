## Overview

The foundational paper that establishes interactive environments stimulate measurable neuroplasticity. It demonstrates that an individual's cognitive adaptation shifts according to how a gameplay loop is configured.

Everything built since — including the tGiX™ algorithm and the Altered Quotient — descends from this initial result.

## Key Findings

- **Neuroplastic Adaptation:** High-resolution interaction loops induce quantifiable neurological adaptations in cognitive processing speed and decision resilience.
- **Structural Influence:** Game structure and rule parameters dictate the nature and direction of cognitive adaptation, proving that interaction design directly modulates mental architecture.
